Why this call?

    One pip = 0.0001, the usual unit for price moves. “Call accuracy” comes from test data the model never saw: calls this strong were right about that many times out of 100 (a coin flip is 50).

    About this model

    What it does

    Estimates the price move of the next EUR/USD 15-minute bar from the shape of the last candle, the time of day, spread and volume. Trained on 100,000 bars from 2021 to 2025.

    How well it works

    On 59,812 bars it had never seen, it called the direction right about 51% of the time. A coin flip scores 50%.

    Why it isn’t a trading signal

    The expected move is usually well under one pip, smaller than the average spread of about 0.5 pip. Trading costs wipe out the edge.

    Educational tool, not investment advice. All calculations run in your browser; no data is sent anywhere.
